## Evidence discipline

Use **project-reported historical**, **implemented with linked checks**, **proposed**, **simulated**, and **pending** as separate statuses. Implementation status must be checked against the eventual study commit: these documents do not certify parallel code changes. At the reviewed snapshot, `AGENTS.md` retained an obsolete Comparator-blocker statement while `docs/verification.md` reported the named challenge pass. The handoff implementation has since corrected `AGENTS.md`; this map records the reviewed snapshot and the scoped report without claiming an independent reproduction.

Every future result table must name its immutable source artifact and regeneration command. Do not infer individual outcomes from aggregates, turn Wilson intervals into a paired significance test, or discard negative cases. The manuscript must link this map and limit its conclusions to evidence actually available. See [protocol](protocol.md) for the proposed controls and denominators.
